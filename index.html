About

I am an Economist at the Czech National Bank, where I head the Markets and Non-Bank Financial Institutions Unit in the Financial Stability Department. I am also a part-time Assistant Professor at the Prague University of Economics and Business (VŠE).

My research lies at the intersection of asset pricing, heterogeneous-agent macroeconomics, and financial markets, with a focus on the interaction between monetary policy and financial stability.

This site provides access to my research, teaching materials, and other academic work. For further details, see my CV and Google Scholar profile. For more light and informal writing, you can check out my Substack.
